# Scientific and software credit

- **Conformal Risk Control:** Anastasios N. Angelopoulos, Stephen Bates, Adam Fisch, Lihua Lei and Tal Schuster. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.02814). The expected-risk method and theorem are theirs; the exact implementation and research evidence packaging here are original authored work. No paper text or upstream implementation is copied.
- **Chákṣu:** J. R. Harish Kumar, Chandra Sekhar Seelamantula, J. H. Gagan, Yogish S. Kamath, Neetha I. R. Kuzhuppilly, U. Vivekanand, Preeti Gupta and Shilpa Patil. *Chákṣu: A glaucoma specific fundus image database*. Scientific Data 10, 70 (2023). [Data descriptor](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-023-01943-4), [version 2 dataset](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.20123135.v2). Credit the dataset creators and the five expert ophthalmologists for the source images and references. Our role assignments, scorer experiment and risk analysis are subsequent research transformations; source assets are not redistributed.
- **Historical scorer architecture:** U-Net (Ronneberger, Fischer and Brox) with ResNet34 (He and coauthors), an ImageNet-pretrained encoder, and the segmentation-models-pytorch software stack. These are established upstream components. This package neither implements that training stack nor distributes its weights or source software.
- **Preserved TypeScript reference:** the original EyeTrustAI development-research implementation of bounded monotone expectation CRC, included under the repository's original-material license. See [reference provenance](reference/README.md). Legacy patient-named fields map to image units for the Chákṣu experiment.

The Chákṣu data license is distinct from this repository's Apache-2.0 license. Citation is scientific attribution; it is not a grant of rights to assets absent from this repository.
